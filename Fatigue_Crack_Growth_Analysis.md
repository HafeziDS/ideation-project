## Why This Problem Matters
When cracks grow in materials like metals, it can make structures such as bridges, cars, and airplanes less safe and lead to early failure. It’s crucial to study how these cracks form and grow so we can improve safety and extend the life of these structures.

By making crack growth analysis easier and more accurate, we can help researchers and students develop stronger, safer materials and designs. This will improve safety in fields like aerospace, automotive, and construction, while also lowering maintenance costs.
